        At the door

        • ✓Say hello and give your name. Check the name on the packet matches.
        • ✓Hand the meal over with both hands.
        • ✓Stay a minute. Ask how they are. For some, you are the only person they speak to today.
        • ✕Don't go inside the home.
        • ✕Don't give money, and don't accept gifts.
        • ✕Don't leave a meal outside an unanswered door.
        Nobody answers? Knock again, call out, wait a moment. Still nothing — tell the organiser or TOUCH straight away. Don't just move on. A senior not answering can be an emergency.
