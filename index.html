Where & when

Getting there Nearest MRT is Bukit Gombak (NS3), then a short bus or walk. Driving? There is parking at the CC — come early, it fills up. Not sure what time to leave home? Check on ontimebo.

Run of show

    Your team

    Each car runs one driver and two to three runners. Driver parks and navigates; runners take the meals to the door.

        Bring these

        0 of 6

          At the door

          • ✓Say hello and give your name. Check the name on the packet matches.
          • ✓Hand the meal over with both hands.
          • ✓Stay a minute. Ask how they are. For some, you are the only person they speak to today.
          • ✕Don't go inside the home.
          • ✕Don't give money, and don't accept gifts.
          • ✕Don't leave a meal outside an unanswered door.
          Nobody answers? Knock again, call out, wait a moment. Still nothing — tell the organiser or TOUCH straight away, and log it above. Don't just move on. A senior not answering can be an emergency.
          Meals are labelled Halal, vegetarian and blended soft meals are not interchangeable. Check the label against the name every time.
